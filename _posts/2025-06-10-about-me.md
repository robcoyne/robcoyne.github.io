I grew up on Cape Cod in southeastern Massachusetts. From there, I attended the [University of Massachusetts Dartmouth](www.umassd.edu) for my undergraduate and masters degree, before going on to pursue a Ph.D. at [the George Washington University](www.gwu.edu). After completion of a post-doc at [Texas Tech University](www.ttu.edu), I started my current position as teaching-track faculty at the [University of Rhode Island](www.uri.edu) where I have been since 2017. 

Professionally, I am an astronomer -- or astrophysicist, if you prefer. My area of expertise is gravitational wave and multi-messenger astronomy, particularly involving gamma-ray bursts. I have been a member of the [LIGO Scientific Collaboration](www.ligo.org) since 2013, and have contributed to most collaboration publications on the topic. I am also active in science communication and public communication, and have served as the chair of the LSC's Communication and Education division. You can read more on my [research page](/research).

In my personal life, I am a proud father of one. I immensely enjoy creative pursuits, and have kept up (albeit intermittently) with numerous artistic hobbies -- especially photography and digital art/graphic design. I may someday share some of those efforts on this website, but for now if you're curious you're welcome to connect with me on my socials or reach out via my [contact page](/contact).
